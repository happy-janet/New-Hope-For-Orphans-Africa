Project Overview

Skills that create dignity, income, and lasting hope.

This planned vocational School will help young people move from dependence toward independence by developing practical talents and work readiness.

2030 Target launch and growth phase

Many young people in vulnerable communities need more than classroom learning. They need practical pathways that help them earn a living, build skills, and become confident contributors to society.

The planned vocational institute will provide training in hands-on skills, entrepreneurship, and productive work life, helping students turn talent into opportunity when the project is launched.

Hands-on learning

Practical training that prepares learners for real work and daily life.

Career preparation

Work-ready skills and guidance to build confidence and income potential.

Long-term impact

Empowering youth with skills that create dignity, independence and future stability.

Training areas

Practical courses for a stronger tomorrow.

The institute will equip young people with useful, marketable skills that support independence and community growth.

Carpentry & Woodwork

Practical training in craftsmanship, repair work, and production for local and household use.

Tailoring & Fashion

Skill-building in sewing, garment making, repair, and entrepreneurship opportunities.

Entrepreneurship

Business and financial literacy designed to support small enterprises and economic growth.

Digital Skills

Foundational computer and communication skills needed for modern work and learning.

Hospitality & Food Service

Hands-on practical training in food preparation, service, and community-based work.

Repair & Maintenance

Technical skills that support everyday productivity and community self-sufficiency.

Expected impact

Building the next generation of capable, productive youth.

This planned project is designed to create pathways toward independence, job readiness, and community transformation.

Project pathway

How the institute will grow.

Phase 1

Planning & design

Finalize site needs, training plans, staffing structure, course selection, and project budgeting.

Phase 2

Construction & setup

Build the institute space, workshops, learning rooms, and essential equipment for practical training.

Phase 3

Training launch

Begin skills programs, mentorship, and practical apprenticeships for young learners.

Phase 4

Growth & sustainability

Expand support into job placement, enterprise development, and long-term community transformation.
