Project Overview

Healthcare access is a foundation for a stronger future.

This clinic is being built to bring reliable medical care closer to families who currently face long distances, high costs, and limited access to treatment.

20K+ People expected to be served yearly

In many communities, children and families live far from hospitals, and treatment is often delayed because of cost, distance, or lack of staff. This project will help address that urgent need by creating a trusted place of care where families can receive timely, compassionate, and quality treatment.

The clinic will support not only emergency care but also prevention, education, and follow-up support, helping the whole community stay healthier and more resilient.

Primary care

General consultations, diagnosis, treatment, health checks, and follow-up support.

Maternal and child health

Safe pregnancy support, newborn care, pediatric treatment, and nutrition guidance.

Emergency support

Immediate triage, urgent care response, and referrals for more complex cases.

What this clinic will provide

Accessible care for children, mothers, and families.

Outpatient care

Regular consultations, treatment of common illnesses, screenings, and follow-up visits for children and adults.

Referral and emergency response

Quick triage and referral pathways for urgent cases, ensuring patients are connected to the right level of care in time.

Community health education

Health awareness sessions on hygiene, illness prevention, maternal health, nutrition, and disease management.
